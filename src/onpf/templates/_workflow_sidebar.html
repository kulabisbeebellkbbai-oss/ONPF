Workflow walkthrough · open or close

Follow these steps in order or open any stage while working. Consulting this guide does not save your current form.

  1. Set up the project. Confirm purpose, local context, activity modules, decision owners, and access in the workspace. Success: the program and assigned members appear there.
  2. Prepare invitations and inquiry questions. Review the questions, scope, intake notice, and recipients before issuing a frozen batch. Success: an issued batch has a number and invitation or print path.
  3. Collect and review responses. Record direct, paper, or meeting input; corrections append revisions. Response coverage shows each current response and review state.
  4. Shape proposals. Link source responses to editable proposals. Saving records incorporation, while generating a draft only fills the form. Success: proposal and source links appear on the refinement board.
  5. Clarify missing details. Issue another numbered round after proposals, decisions, or during document review. Keep each question's source and reason visible, then record answers or explicit deferrals. A new round can follow any earlier round.
  6. Record owner decisions. Owners inspect proposal evidence, add exceptions, and save outcomes and rationale. A decision is separate from document or release approval.
  7. Draft every required document. Open each document from the workspace, review linked decisions, and save each section. Create recipe cards, posters, and other program media in additional documents when needed. Success: the workspace shows all linked sections drafted; this still needs owner review.
  8. Review evidence and operating permission. Check coverage, group review drafts, public wording, document consistency, and any site permission and copy distribution. Missing permission remains unresolved even if design text mentions it.
  9. Prepare and approve. Owners prepare an exact-content candidate, check its hash and frozen context, and explicitly approve. Changes require a new candidate. Design approval does not prove permission to operate.
  10. Publish or export. Review literal public content for privacy, choose the intended public version, and download its documents or package. Publication is an explicit act independent of design approval.

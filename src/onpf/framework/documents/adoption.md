# Adoption guide

## local-decisions | Local decisions and dependencies
List permissions, local requirements, unresolved decisions, and dependencies another agency must check.

## optional-elements | Optional elements
Explain which activities are optional and how to adapt them.

## adaptation-history | Adaptation history
Record sources, material versions, and local changes; leave unknown history explicit.

## release-contents | Release contents and notices
Identify the intended package, third-party notices, and MIT-0 licensing for project-owned material. No approval is implied by a draft.

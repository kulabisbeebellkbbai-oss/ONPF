# Facilitating a program design session

Create accounts locally using the README account command. For custom, demo or restored storage, use `create-user --database PATH --username USER` with the same database as the running service, and its `--instance PATH` before the command. Create a program with a working title, purpose, local context and relevant inquiry modules. Assign persisted accounts as decision owners or facilitators. Owners control membership, decision fields and approval rules; facilitators can collect and draft. Keep operating permission pending until the appropriate organization actually confirms it. A document release and operating permission are separate records.

Use development stages to select independent questions together. Conditional questions become available only when their prerequisite owner decision fields are set. Issue a batch to freeze its question wording/version. Editing the question catalog later does not alter a batch that participants have already received. Each question accepts several responses, including different or opposing perspectives. Participants can answer, abstain, mark not applicable or skip. Do not require agreement or a single group answer.

For direct input, create an invitation from the frozen batch and share the complete link privately with intended participants. The token is in a URL fragment, exchanged by the page and removed from its visible URL. An invitation grants access to that questionnaire, not to the private program or other people's answers. The receipt gives a reference and count only. Revoke the invitation or close the batch when collection ends. Browser CSRF protections must remain enabled.

For paper or meeting input, open the batch's print view and print its question references and writing space. Use Letter at 100% and, if printing both sides, flip on the long edge; check your own print preview before collecting a session. The test browser could not verify native print pagination. Transcribe through your facilitator account, selecting paper or meeting as the entry method. Select named, alias, or anonymous attribution independently of the person entering the record. Use a participant's chosen label only with their consent. Keep optional quotation permission separate; public exports exclude raw responses and contributor details even when quotation permission is recorded. Review owner-authored draft text for personal details before sharing it.

Add another response to preserve a second perspective. A network retry of the same form is deduplicated; two intentionally submitted identical responses remain separate records. A duplicate marker retains both entries. Open private response history to append a contributor-requested correction with its reason. Original text remains visible and review reopens. If someone corrected it while your form was open, your draft stays in the form: compare the displayed current revision, revise your draft as needed, then explicitly acknowledge that revision before appending. Another intervening correction requires another review.

Group responses manually into linked proposals. For each current response, record incorporated, adapted, deferred or declined, with an explanation and supporting proposals where needed. Inclusion in the input record does not imply adoption. Preserve disagreement and explain owner choices. Record an owner decision and select its support when saving a document section. Unreviewed input prevents preparation of a new candidate.

Share selected documents as a frozen review draft only after checking the literal document text for personal information. It excludes raw input, account controls and internal rationales; revoke its link when review ends. Edits afterward do not silently update that frozen review.

Recorded removal prevents creating a new review containing known copied text in its title or selected documents. Clean the selected text/title or select unaffected documents; unrelated internal decisions and unselected documents do not need cleanup merely to share a clean review. Create a new link for the clean selection. A previously revoked link stays unavailable.

When ready, prepare an exact candidate. Owners inspect its document snapshot, unresolved requirements, selected materials, coverage, change notes, version and hash. Each designated owner explicitly approves under the chosen all/any rule; consensus is optional. Content, owners or rules changing during approval makes the pending candidate stale. Scoped response corrections invalidate it too. Late input is visibly counted for the next cycle. Re-review and prepare a new candidate when needed; do not assume an earlier approval covers new content.

The resulting release is immutable. Its release number, exact snapshot, chosen shared-material versions and approvals remain identifiable. Unresolved drafts stay visibly incomplete; an approval is not certification of readiness or permission to operate. Export the approved program for reuse. Continue later work as drafts and create a new independently approved release. Use the private backup guide before actual collection and agree on retention, administrator access and correction/removal handling.
